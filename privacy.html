BrightBooks Privacy Policy

Published by Sulitek Studio. Contact: sulitekstudio@gmail.com

BrightBooks is a children's storybook app. We respect children's privacy and collect as little as possible.

What the app keeps

  • A child's nickname, avatar, age, reading level and favourite genres
  • Favourite books
  • Reading time, streaks, badges, stars and stickers
  • App settings such as language, narration, dark mode and text size

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the device. We do not ask for a child's real name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Stories

The app downloads published stories from our online library. This does not send any profile or progress data.

Ads and purchases

If ads or in-app purchases are added, they will follow Google Play's Families Policy. Ads will be child-appropriate and never shown while a story is being read. Purchases are handled by Google Play, and we do not receive payment details.

Deleting data

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes all data kept by the app.

Parents

Parents can ask questions or make requests at sulitekstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
